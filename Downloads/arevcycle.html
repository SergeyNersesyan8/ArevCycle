The fund covers members' share of all panel waste by market share, the same way European schemes handle panels sold before a scheme existed.

Does a level fee carry the fund through the 2030s?

Waste grows every year, so the cost per year grows too. A level fee collects more than needed early on and builds a ring fenced reserve for the wave that follows.

Book a pickup, follow the batch, get a certificate

Plants and installers hand over broken panels. Every batch is tracked to the recycler, and the certificate proves the panels were handled as hazardous waste should be.
